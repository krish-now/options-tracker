ORB

SPX's opening range, 60-minute and 30-minute: each range is traded on its own, with its own trade a day and its own P/L. Until 12:00 ET, on a day the market closes at 16:00 and whose range is at least 0.2% of the open: once SPX trades above the range (and never below it), sell a 5-wide put spread with its short at the first strike under the range low; once it trades below the range (and never above it), a call spread over the range high. 5 contracts at the mid less $0.05, only if that is at least $0.50 ($0.60 on the 30-minute range). One trade a day per range, held to expiry and settled against SPX's close. Once open, a 5-minute close through the long strike sells the same-width spread on the other side, short on the same strike - an iron fly - if that takes at least $0.80. Paper; no commission.
